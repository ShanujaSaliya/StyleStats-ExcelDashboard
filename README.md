# StyleStats Dashboard — Excel Project

🛍 **Objective**: Analyze annual sales for 2023 to understand customer behavior and drive stronger growth in 2024.  

📈 **Solution**: Implemented an advanced **Excel-based StyleStats Dashboard** with interactive charts, KPIs, and filters.  

---

## 🔎 Questions answered (analysis list)

The dashboard answers the following business questions:

- 📊 What is the **total sales amount per month**?  
- 👥 How many **orders were placed by each age group**?  
- 🏷️ How many **products of each category** were sold?  
- 📏 Which **product size** has the **highest total sales amount** across all categories?  
- 🔄 How do **cancelled, refunded, and returned quantities** vary month-wise?  
- 🌐 Which **categories are most popular in each channel** ?  

---

## ✅ Final Conclusion  

The **StyleStats Dashboard** reveals key sales insights from 2023, highlighting top categories, customer age groups, product sizes, and channel performance. It also tracks cancellations, refunds, and returns to identify operational gaps.  

👉 By focusing on best-selling products, optimizing channels, and reducing returns, StyleStats can make **data-driven decisions** to boost growth and customer satisfaction in 2024.
